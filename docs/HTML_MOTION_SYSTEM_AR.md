## Original CP motion grammar
Fast press 110ms; page 230ms; sheet 280ms; poster 380ms; layout 260ms; close 240ms. Suggested independent defaults, not measured Nuvio timings. Duration multiplier 50–150%, user's reduced motion or hidden => 0. Transform/opacity FLIP inverse bounds via WAAPI, avoid continuous RAF and repeated layout reads per frame. On close/focus restore; one overlay at a time, cancel pending animations and handle finished rejection. Long press 460ms/12px threshold in lab, movement/cancel kills timer, ordinary tap accessible, Shift+F10 alternative; validate touch/IME/back in real Android. No irreversible actions coupled to animation. Real operations only after native ACK; simulated action here. 
Three continue layouts: landscape card, wide compact row, portrait poster; same stable identity/progress and selection. Section hidden when empty or disabled; visibility never deletes history. Individual remove, watched, restart, details, close; production preserve explicit bulk remove by show/season/range. Overlay anchored to poster, backdrop blur toggle, bottom sheet when wide. Real shared-image transition to details, swipe-to-dismiss, undo, hero crossfade, native back, transition interruption/rotation and dock adaptive remain further work. In dense downloads avoid animated reflow and blur; bounded rows, progress only.
